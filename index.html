        Small communities. Big conversations.

        All the ways to yap.

        A familiar sub-yap, with the details that make a community feel like home.

        More than a post.

        Text, links, images, videos, and polls. Threaded replies, crossposts, post flair, and AMAs keep the conversation going in every direction.

        A feed that feels like you.

        Join sub-yaps, follow people, and build custom feeds. Sort by what’s hot, new, or top. Search, save a good find, or take a conversation to private messages and chat.

        Your community. Your call.

        Moderators set the rules, pin important posts, manage flair and wikis, review reports, remove content, and handle bans. Mod mail and moderation logs keep teams on the same page.
